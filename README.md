# Track 1 Day 25 — TutorAI Vietnam Monetization

**Project:** TutorAI Vietnam — AI study workspace for Vietnamese university students

## Deliverables

- `HoangBaoHuy_Day25_model.xlsx` — five-tab, formula-driven monetization model.
- `HoangBaoHuy_Day25_onepager.pdf` — single-page monetization summary.

## Decision summary

TutorAI uses a **hybrid** value metric: 199,000 VND/month for 30 study credit requests and 69,000 VND per extra 15 requests. It is not outcome-priced because TutorAI has not yet established attribution for learning outcomes. The first 90-day channel is **PLG via LMS/web**, at the pre-quiz study moment; sales-led and partner-led are deliberately out of scope.

The model uses 30 attempts/month × 80% planning containment = 24 completed jobs. Direct monthly COGS is 54,000 VND: API 30,000, infrastructure 8,000, sampled QA/HITL 6,000, retry reserve 3,000 and allocated overhead 7,000. That produces a Cost/Job of 2,250 VND and a plan gross margin of 72.9%. The conservative 60% GM containment gate is 67.8%.

## Evidence posture

The 80% containment, Day 24 API allowance, 500,000 VND CAC and 199,000 VND ARPU are labelled assumptions, not measured TutorAI results. Day 21 is used only as an evaluation-method reference; no other student’s results are claimed as Huy’s evidence.
